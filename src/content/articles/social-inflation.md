The more freely we spend affectionate words, the less they mean.

Words are meant to reflect reality—not manufacture it.  
  
Reserve weighty expressions for when they are true, and they retain their weight.  
  
Perhaps we don't know the weight of these words in the first place. They just "sound nice".
