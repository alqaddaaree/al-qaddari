In the Name of Allah, and blessings and peace be upon His Messenger.

To proceed:

Sheikh Maahir bin Thaafer Al-Qahtaanee – may Allah preserve him – mentioned about his wife – may Allah have mercy on her, Sukayna bint Al-Albaany – that she had a blog called **“Tamaam Al-Minnah”**.

  
We searched for it and found it: [Tamaam Al-Minnah Blog](https://www.blogger.com/#)  
 

May Allah bless it. It is a treasure, an extension of Al-Albaany’s fiqh in da’wah. A rare and precious fiqh, in a time when spreading knowledge has become an end rather than a means. And Allah is the one whose help is sought.

Here is the translation of the note she pinned on her blog’s main page:

****

> **Notice**
> 
> _I do not have any page or account on any of what is called social media: Facebook, Twitter, Google Plus, WhatsApp, etc., and I have never subscribed to any of them at any time since they started. In general, I only write on this blog: (Tamaam Al-Minnah), and the blog shared with my sister Hassaana – who is like me in this regard –: ([Kitaab Ad-Daleel ila Kayfiyyat Ta’leem Al-Qur'aan Al-Kareem](https://www.blogger.com/#))._

__

Some might think this woman had not heard the statement of Al-Uthaymeen: _“Contend with the people of falsehood on the Internet until the truth prevails”_ , and they are far from the mark.

We were very pleased that we agreed with her method, as we placed the following excerpt from [our book Al-Buzugh](http://arm-alqaddari.codeberg.page/bools/buzoogh/) on the front page of our own website:

### 

> ### A Spark of Hope
> 
> However, praise be to Allah, I admire how some scholars establish their own websites, controlling them as they wish.

> The means have developed, and we have young specialists in programming, hardware, and software, which allow us to dispense with corrupt websites.  
>  This form of contending is what can serve as an interpretation and application of Imaam Ibn Uthaymeen’s statement – may Allah have mercy on him –.  
>  An application that gives Muslims a special position and presence on the internet.  
>  An application that fulfills the Prophet’s – peace be upon him – guidance in opposing the disbelievers and not imitating them.  
>  An application in which no major sins are committed.  
>  An application free of innovation.  
>  An application that filters out followers of whims and lovers of fame.  
>  An application on the path of the Book, the Sunnah, and the righteous predecessors.

Some, may Allah guide them, accuse this of being rigidity or extremism, and that it is fleeing from confronting the people of falsehood, or restricting knowledge to books and centers.  
May Allah forgive you. Even if that were our statement, we know that the knowledge of the Salaf only spread through those two means.

In reality, this exposes their true understanding, which is the same as that of those who chant “keeping up with the modern agr” and not “being rigid in old methods”, yet they are unaware of this.

From Allaah we seek help with the scourge of images, social media, and visual content platforms.
