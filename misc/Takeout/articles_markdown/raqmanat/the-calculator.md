Think of AI as a calculator.

  
A calculator doesn't understand math, it only follows rules, processes inputs, to then give outputs.  
  
You don't ask a calculator what to calculate; it asks you that.  
  
So if you don't know the question, the calculator and its answers are useless.  
  
The same is true for AI.  
  
AI relies on three Ps: It patterns. It predicts. It produces.  
  
That's it.  
  
Just like a calculator doesn't make you a mathematician. AI doesn't make you a builder.  
  
But a mathematician with a calculator?  
A builder with AI?  
  
!  
  
A tool is a tool.  
  
The problem is when people treat the output as wisdom instead of mechanical arithmetic.  
  
Think of someone flexing AI output as someone flexing calculator output.  
  
If you're lazy, AI will amplify that. Lazy Pro Max.  
If you're creative, AI will amplify that. Creative Pro Max.  
If you're chaotic, AI will amplify that. Chaotic Pro Max.  
  
AI is not some magical, weird new tool that emerged out of thin air. It's the same concept of automation and amplification we witness in this era of technology.
