This is a translation of "دروس جامع مسائل العقيدة الصحيحة" (Duroos Jaami' Masaa’il al-Aqeedah as-Saheeha). The original series is by Dr. Faysal bin Misfir az-Zinaamy al-Waadi’y. Translation by Abdurrahmaan Meehoob al-Qaddaary, with permission graciously granted by the author. 

 

The original audio lessons were transcribed using software, then translated and rephrased for smoother English flow. Finally, the translations were recorded with some improvisation.
