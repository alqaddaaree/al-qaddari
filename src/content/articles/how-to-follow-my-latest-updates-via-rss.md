If you want to keep up with my latest blogs without checking multiple sites or relying on social media, the best way is to use an **RSS Feed**.

Think of it as a "Personalized News Stream" where new posts from all my blogs arrive in one single place.

#### 1\. Choose Your Reader: Feedly

While there are many "RSS Readers" out there, I recommend **[Feedly](https://feedly.com)** for beginners. It is free, works on both web browsers and [mobile phones](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.devhd.feedly), and is very simple to set up.

#### 2\. How to Add My Blogs

Once you have created a free Feedly account, click on **"Add Content"** (the Wifi and **+** icon) and simply paste the links below into the search bar:

  * **Kids Channel:** `https://islaamchildrenbooks.blogspot.com/`

  * **Personal Website:** `https://arm-alqaddari.codeberg.page`

  * **Arabic Blog:** `https://armalqaddaaree.blogspot.com`

  * **English Blog:** `https://en-armalqaddaaree.blogspot.com`

  * **Photography (Arabic):** `https://fitnattasweer.blogspot.com`

  * **Photography (English):** `https://scourgeofphotography.blogspot.com`
